# Personal Website Design Guide

## Concept

The website combines two visual ideas:

1. The precision and futuristic character of next-generation intelligent semiconductors
2. The human-centered innovation of digital healthcare

The overall mood should feel intelligent, calm, modern, and trustworthy rather than flashy.

## Color System

- Deep navy background: `#06111F`
- Soft navy surface: `#0B1C2E`
- Primary blue: `#5CA9FF`
- Healthcare mint accent: `#42E8C4`
- Main text: `#F4F8FB`
- Secondary text: `#A7B7C7`

Blue represents semiconductor and computing technology. Mint represents healthcare, life, and possibility.

## Typography

- Use a clean Korean sans-serif system font stack.
- Headlines should be large, bold, and tightly spaced.
- Body text should have generous line height for readability.
- Small technical labels may use a monospace font.

## Layout

- Use a maximum content width of approximately `1120px`.
- Keep generous vertical whitespace between sections.
- Use an asymmetric two-column hero layout on desktop.
- Collapse all multi-column layouts into one column on mobile.

## Components

- Sticky translucent navigation bar
- Large introduction hero section
- Profile card with initials instead of a required photo
- Two interest cards, with the first-choice major visually emphasized
- Direction statement and concise current goal
- Clear email contact section

## Visual Details

- Use a subtle circuit-like grid in the background.
- Use translucent navy cards and thin blue or mint borders.
- Keep border radii between `20px` and `30px` for a modern but composed style.
- Use gradients only for emphasis, such as the main phrase and primary buttons.
- Hover effects should be restrained and should not interfere with reading.

## Accessibility and Responsive Behavior

- Maintain sufficient contrast between text and background.
- Include visible keyboard focus through the existing hover/focus styles.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Ensure the page remains readable on screens narrower than `560px`.

## Content Principle

- Do not exaggerate experience or claim completed projects that do not exist.
- Present the student honestly as a first-year undergraduate exploring future majors.
- Clearly distinguish the first-choice field, next-generation intelligent semiconductors, from the second-choice field, digital healthcare.
